## 1. Why rounds 3–5 found nothing

Short version; INV §2 and §3 (RC4) have the detail.
- **Round 3, `prc_local` (62032412).**
  - It added one blind offset to every threshold of a population and carried no loss signal.
  - Moves were 0.9–2% of cycles, non-local, and skipped up to 13 rungs.
  - 19/20 candidates were worse on search. Tests: +0.212%, +0.342%, −0.030%.
- **Round 4, `prc_adjacent` (62104342, 4B/llama8B t32) and its 30B companion (62105189, 30B t32/t40).**
  - Moves were one-rung, but only 0.005–0.068% of cycles.
  - That is below the per-call re-dispatch cascade each edit triggers (median 3–15× the planned move) and below the 0.5–1.1%
    confirmation MDE.
  - The one confirmation pass, llama8B t32, lost on test (+0.077%). Best(all) is unchanged.
- **Common cause.** No generator used Fisher, error or loss information, and the step sizes were wrong in both directions.
  The evaluator is not at fault: 16-window held-out vs full test gives r = 0.96–0.97.
- **Structural limits no round can move (INV RC1, RC2, RC5):**
  - The dense t32–t48 residual is per-token loss sensitivity that no runtime statistic sees. Deployable headroom is
    ≤0.25–0.5% at t32.
  - t96 is within 0.35–0.67% of the SC ceiling.
  - T1's per-group lever reaches only the dispatched linears, 51–77% of SC cycles at t32.
- **What is still live:**
  - **RC3.** On 30B t32–t48, qk sits at the inherited ladder top (96/96/109), and av l3 is also top-bound on t40/t48.
  - **RC6.** 14B t32 and 30B t64 under-spend the parent cost (−1.34% / −0.64%) and sit 0.07% / 0.05% above ≤1.05× fp16.
  - **CRIT §1 (new).** On 30B the joint λ's family exchange rate is wrong:
    - κ = measured / MC-Fisher-predicted ΔNLL. κ_lin is 1.43–2.08 on all five 30B budgets, on both held-out and test;
      pooled held-out κ_lin ≈ 1.96.
    - κ_att ≈ 0.70 / 1.02 / 0.70 at t32/t40/t48, pooled ≈ 0.83. So κ_att/κ_lin ≈ 0.42.
    - The joint λ therefore trades attention against linears at an exchange rate that is off by 1.6–2.9×, and it leaves av
      over-funded relative to linears.
  - The κ correction is not applied to dense cells, where the joint increments are too small to price attention.
