## Verdict

1. **The dense conclusion survives.** For 4B t32 (needs −1.25% to ≤1.10×), llama8B t32/t40 (−4.17/−1.15%) and all 14B cells I found no
   pure-allocation lever with a plausible ≥0.5%. The best dense item left is Q6 (efficient family transfers) at 0–0.4%.
2. **On 30B the investigation mis-specified the joint currency.** The MC-Fisher *under*-predicts linear moves by about 2×: κ_lin is
   1.43–2.08 on 5 of 5 30B budgets, on both held-out and test. It *over*-predicts the attention increment: κ_att ≈ 0.70 / 1.02 / 0.70 at
   t32/t40/t48. So the joint λ trades attention against linears at an exchange rate that is off by **1.6–2.9×**. RC3/H1 tested raise-vs-cut
   residuals, not the relative price of the two families, so the investigation never drew this out.
3. **Three consequences:**
   - (a) **A new lever:** a family-calibrated joint λ. It is pure allocation (a change of calibration objective) and needs no new rungs.
     A toy sizing gives 0.2–0.8% on 30B t32–t48.
   - (b) **Round A as written (unpin qk to 128 in the uncorrected currency) will over-shoot on 30B.** Its kill bar of −0.01 nats is wrong
     in both directions. 30B t32 needs about −0.016 to −0.021 nats just to beat giving the same cycles to linears. 4B t64 only needs about
     −0.0011 to −0.002.
   - (c) A linear counterfactual (arm B, or a measured linear elasticity) is **mandatory**. Without it no gross attention arm can be
     interpreted.
4. **Revised expectations:**
   - 20-cell mean: −0.1 to −0.35 pp. The investigation said −0.05 to −0.3.
   - 30B t32/t40/t48: 0.2–1.2% each if κ-correction, unpinning and per-block attention keys stack.
   - Threshold flips: 30B t40 to ≤1.10× (needs −1.21%) is possible but below 50%. 14B t32 to ≤1.05× is likely, via the retarget. No
     other threshold flips.
5. **No remaining lever has plausible ≥0.5% upside on llama8B or 14B.** So no standalone trial meets `feedback_no_known_outcome_trials`
   ("genuine upside on ALL 4 models"). Package the 30B work as one algorithm update, "joint λ with a family-calibrated currency over
   ladders that reach L_max". Define it for all 20 cells, but spend GPU only where the offline solve predicts at least the MDE.

**Pooled over 30B** (held-out only, so no test leakage):
- κ_lin = Σmeasured/Σpredicted over t32–t96 = 0.1631/0.0834 = **1.96**.
- κ_att ≈ **0.83**, weighted by predicted gain.
- **κ_att/κ_lin ≈ 0.42.**

**How κ_att was decomposed.**
- The joint increment is the attention gain minus the linear cut.
- The linear cut's predicted loss is E_gfis·((L_par/L_gfisla)^p − 1). Linear L comes from the calib7 logs:
  - t32: 23.93→23.50;
  - t40: 34.83→31.65;
  - t48: 43.06→37.64.
- The linear cut is re-priced at κ_lin, and κ_att is solved from the measured net.
- On t32 and t48 the conclusion holds at ±1 SE: κ_att stays at or below 0.9 while κ_lin stays at or above 1.6.

**Dense cells.**
- κ_lin is scattered:
  - 4B 1.84/1.41/0.97/1.49/0.40;
  - llama8B 1.58/1.40/0.76/0.98/1.48;
  - 14B 1.17/0.70/1.08/1.01/0.80.
- The dense joint increments are too small to price attention: |pred| ≤ 0.006 nats against an SE of 0.002–0.005. So **do not apply a κ
  correction to dense cells.** 4B t40 and t64 give ratios of 0.96 and 1.65, both within noise.

**Weak supporting evidence.**
- In R3 on 30B t40 (`prc_local_20260926/30B_t40/search_*_nll.json`, 6 windows), the directional term qk←linears vs linears←qk is
  +0.27%: moving budget toward linears was better.
- It is z ≈ 0.3, so it is consistent with the finding but not significant.

**Caveat.**
- κ_lin is measured on a *reallocation* (parent→gfis, mostly granularity plus the global split), not on a pure linear-budget shift. The
  decisive check is c17 vs c17_s80 on the same windows; see L3.
- `30B_t{32,40,48}_c17_s80_table.json` exist on Turbo, and no 30B s80 has ever been evaluated.

**What it means for the allocation.**
- av is not pinned; round 2 cut it to 28–60. At the joint optimum, av's Fisher marginal equals λ. So av's *true* marginal is about 0.8λ,
  against about 1.9λ for linears. By that measure, av on 30B t32/t48 is still over-funded relative to linears.
- qk is pinned at 96/96/109, so whether its true marginal exceeds κ_lin·λ is unknown. Settling that requires the per-bucket attention
  curves, which no artifact holds: `--dump` writes linears only.

- **Attention ladder interior density** (dense rungs where av mass sits, beyond adding a top).

    a 17-rung ladder. That is ≤0.1–0.15% PPL.
  - Include dense rungs in the capture because they are free, but they are not a round.
- **Table lottery / selection among surrogate-equivalent tables (Q4).**
  - True between-table spread from the 13 c6/c7 held-out pairs: observed variance 0.124 vs mean window-SE² 0.102 (%²). That gives
    σ ≈ 0.15% per table *difference*, or 0.10% per table. Σz² is 17.0 on 13 df.
  - Best-of-4 expected value is ≤0.15%, and it is pure test-selection inflation. Drop Q4.
- **Cross-chunk covariance / second-order terms.**
  - `error_curves_w` sums per-chunk squared error and drops the 2Σε_cε_c′ terms.
  - For uncorrelated chunk partial sums this is unbiased. Only a coherent SC shrinkage bias would matter, and no artifact measures one.
  - Hypothesis only, not a round.
- **Linear rows-per-call and calibration windows.** LOWO and the c6/c7 pairs bound linear table variance at ≈0.1%.
- **30B expert sampling (16/128).** It is re-weighted without bias (`rep *= n_units/expert_calls`, calib7 :385), and cost and error scale

- **Escape μ/τ recalibration.** ≤0.1%. It is moot on buckets whose top becomes 128, and grey in the paper's framing.
- **Deliberate +1% cycles.** Known outcome: e = 0.19–0.44 gives +0.2–0.44% at t32. It flips nothing.
- **Dense-vs-causal attention cost model.** This is accounting, not allocation.
- **Per-expert keys, absolute thresholds, linear direction or statistic, per-op psl.** Each is new metadata or a runtime-rule change.

## 3. Audit of Rounds A/B/C and the §6 questions

**Round A**
- **Compliant.** The ladder max is ≤128, RNG is untouched (`_attn_grid_for` returns None), and there is no qk rebalance or escape-code
  change. The validator must assert max ≤128 itself, because `_extract_group_levels` does not check it (scout §0.6).
- **"No κ_att multiplier" is wrong for 30B** (§1). RC3's refutation of leg (b) covers raise-vs-cut residuals only.
- **The gate "hold prediction ≤ −0.5% after a 0.6× discount" uses one discount for both families.**
  - Example: a solve predicting an attention gain of −0.020 and a linear cut of +0.008 (net −1.2%) passes (−0.72%).
  - At κ_att 0.8 and κ_lin 1.9 that candidate is ≈ +0.1%, i.e. a loss.
  - Use family κ: pred_true = κ_att·ΔA + κ_lin·ΔL.
- **The kill rule "gross ΔNLL(A) > −0.01" is not cell-specific.** The value of the same extra cycles given to linears (κ_lin × Fisher
  linear error × (1 − (1+Δ)^−p), from diag E_gfis and the scout's cycle shares) is:

  | cell / arm | extra cycles | linear alternative (nats) |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | 30B t32 A_inv | +6.95% | **0.016–0.021** (−0.01 is too lenient) |
  | 30B t40 A_inv | +8.60% | 0.0075–0.010 |
  | 4B t40 | +9.0% | 0.007–0.009 |
  | 4B t64 | +4.22% | **0.0011–0.0020** (−0.01 kills a possibly real win; measured 4B t64 c17 vs c17_s80 test elasticity 0.045 gives 0.0018) |
  | 30B ATT128 | +44.6% (t32) / +29.1% (t40) | 0.058–0.068 / 0.018–0.022 |

  So "ΔNLL(ATT128) > −0.01 ⇒ no attention round can pay" is only a necessary condition, and a very weak one.
- **The ≥85%-on-top bucket rule is unnecessary.** A joint solve over a superset ladder cannot do worse on the calibration windows, so give
  every qk/av bucket rungs up to 128 and let the DP choose. The scout already found that the rule and the enumerated list disagree on
  30B (+4.42% vs +8.60% at t40).
- **The scope is 30B + 4B.** Under the all-4-models rule, frame it as a component of one algorithm, not as a 30B round.

**Round B**
- **Sub-1% retargets break `feedback_ppl_over_cost`** ("don't burn GPU chasing sub-1% cost precision"): 14B t40 (−0.94%) and 30B t64
  (−0.64%). Keep only 14B t32 (−1.34%) and 30B t48 (−1.02%, piggybacked on L1).
- **Drop psl ±1 step on 14B t48.** It is not a weak cell, and the expectation is ≤0.15%. llama8B t40 psl should be a held-out screen, not
  a full test.
- **The target definition is correct**: held-out-train child cost, fixed point, no test data.

**Round C**

- Q5: fit8 bound ≤0.25–0.5% in-sample, realistic ≤0.2%. Optional filler.
- Q6: keep (L5). It is the only dense item whose ≥0.5% is not excluded.
- Q10: keep, but for framing only. It adds no PPL. 4B t32 is enough; the 30B t40 control costs about 6 GPU-h for a caveat.

## 4. §5d user decisions: recommended defaults

   - Retarget to exactly parent cost only where under-spend is >1% (14B t32, 30B t48). Leave sub-1% drift alone.

   - It is a runtime-rule change (`feedback_runtime_free_no_new_hardware`, and the paper's per-call-relative dispatch).
   - The Fisher-currency evidence is negative.
4. **Dense attention cost model.** Default **keep dense accounting.**
   - Causal pricing would redefine iso-cost for parent and child mid-rebuttal.
   - State "attention MACs counted dense (incl. causally masked positions)" in the cost statement.
