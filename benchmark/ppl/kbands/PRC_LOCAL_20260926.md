The pipeline automatically:

1. Checks frozen inputs and loads the incumbent into one GPU.
2. Selects six TRAIN search windows and sixteen fresh confirmation windows,
   excluding known earlier calibration/held-out windows. Corpus sampling uses a
   local Python RNG; SC RNG state is not reseeded or changed.
3. Verifies identical incumbent NLL and exact traced compute with and without
   observe-only profiling. Profiles include true tail-chunk widths, protected
   slices, attention escape rows, and all executed experts.
4. Generates up to eight bidirectional transfers: QK/linears, AV/linears, QK/AV,
   and attention projections/MLP. Each transfers at most 2% of total SC cycles.
   Pinned populations can reduce the number of feasible candidates. There is one
   search iteration, preserving the small pilot scope.
5. Evaluates complete candidates on identical search windows; filters by exact
   traced cost within 1% of the incumbent. Reports paired NLL differences.
6. Checks the best new feasible candidate and incumbent on fresh confirmation
   windows. Confirmation quality is recorded independently. The new candidate
   receives one full TEST evaluation if its confirmation cost remains within 1%,
   even if confirmation loss did not win.
7. Verifies full-test token count/context/stride/batch/RNG settings, records exact
   cost, and compares with the frozen incumbent. A worse or non-comparable result
   cannot replace the incumbent. Final reporting remains best across comparable
   full-test allocation rounds.
